Overview

At RecoveryAI, we value your privacy and trust. RecoveryAI provides AI-powered conversational SMS communication, missed-call recovery, lead capture, customer follow-up, booking coordination, and customer support communication tools designed for SMB (Small and Medium-sized Businesses) partners.

Our platform helps businesses respond faster to customers, recover missed opportunities, improve communication workflows, and provide streamlined customer support through direct phone and SMS interactions.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your information.

2

Information We Collect

To provide our communication and workflow services effectively, we may collect the following information:

Contact Data

Name, phone number, and related customer contact details.

SMS Message Content

Full historical log of conversation content exchanged through the platform.

Call Metadata

Missed call timestamps, caller numbers, and associated connection records.

Service Request Details

Information related directly to booking requests, business inquiries, and support logs.

Preferences

Scheduling preferences, specific service booking dates, and notification flags.

Business Settings

Internal message dispatch rules, team permissions, and workflow configurations.

Website & Device Information

Browser type, device classification, IP addresses, and anonymous analytical interactions with our landing properties.

3

How We Use Information

The information we collect is utilized strictly to secure, improve, analyze, and operate the RecoveryAI ecosystem. Specifically, we process this details to:

  • Respond instantly to customer queries on behalf of our client SMBs.
  • Transmit AI conversational follow-up messages automatically.
  • Actively recover missed-call opportunities to re-engage potential leads.
  • Coordinate calendars, service schedules, and real-time business appointment pipelines.
  • Deliver functional, reliable, and secure end-user client support.
  • Enhance overall platform reliability, architectural speed, and digital safeguards.
  • Identify, block, and prevent fraudulent activity, system spam, or workspace misuse.
  • Maintain overall service operational performance metrics and reporting data dashboards.

SMS Communications

By submitting an inquiry form, requesting more details, scheduling online appointments, dialing directly or texting the phone number of a business utilizing RecoveryAI, you consent to receive related automated SMS communications from that business through our software platform.

Message Frequency

Transmissions frequency directly corresponds to user activity, query status, or specific custom service operations requested.

Message & Data Rates

Depending entirely on standard service agreement clauses set by your specific cellular carrier, standard text or transfer data rates may apply.

Opt-Out & Help

Text the keyword STOP to immediately drop any communication sequence, or HELP for immediate guidance.

Data Sharing & Third-Party

At RecoveryAI, user data absolute confidentiality is protected. Under no standard operation do we offer, broker, lease, or distribute private customer lists for marketing actions.

Strict SMS Compliance Statement

No mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in consent records, or consumer personal information will be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for marketing, promotional, or advertising purposes under any circumstances.

Permitted Core Infrastructure Outflows:

Intended Business

The original small or local SMB business entity that the customer explicitly initiated interaction with.

Core Vendors

Essential technology providers, cloud database operators, and messaging bridges required to execute core program activities.

Legal Mandates

Instances requiring compliance with court orders, regulatory subpoenas, valid judicial requests, or legal regulations.
