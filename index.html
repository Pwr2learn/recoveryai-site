Overview

At RecoveryAI, we value your privacy and trust. RecoveryAI provides AI-powered conversational SMS communication, missed-call recovery, lead capture, customer follow-up, booking coordination, and customer support communication tools designed for SMB (Small and Medium-sized Businesses) partners.

Our platform helps businesses respond faster to customers, recover missed opportunities, improve communication workflows, and provide streamlined customer support through direct phone and SMS interactions.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your information.

Contact Data

Name, phone number, and related customer contact details.

Service Request Details

Information related directly to booking requests, business inquiries, and support logs.

Scheduling preferences, specific service booking dates, and notification flags.

Business Settings

Internal message dispatch rules, team permissions, and workflow configurations.
