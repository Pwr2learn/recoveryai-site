Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 18, 2026 • Scope: Platform & Messaging Users

1 Overview

At RecoveryAI, we value your privacy and trust. RecoveryAI provides an AI-powered SMS communication, missed-call recovery, lead capture, customer follow-up, and service request support designed for SMB businesses and customer communication workflows.

By helping businesses capture missed opportunities and establish streamlined communication channels with customers, our platform facilitates prompt, conversational, and effective interactions via phone and text message. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, handle, and protect your information.

2 Information We Collect

To provide our AI-driven communication services, we may collect the following information:

  • Contact Data: Name and phone number.
  • SMS Message Content: Text history exchanged through the platform.
  • Call Metadata: Log details such as missed call time and phone numbers.
  • Service Details: Information regarding service or booking requests.
  • Preferences: Booking, scheduling, and custom follow-up preferences.
  • Business Settings: Internal communication configurations of our partner SMBs.
  • Website & Device Info: Basic browser type, device type, and anonymous interaction metrics if you visit our corporate website.

3 How We Use Information

The information we collect is strictly used to deliver high-quality platform services. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Respond automatically and intelligently to immediate customer inquiries.
  • Initiate automated missed-call follow-up messages on behalf of service businesses.
  • Efficiently capture leads and service request information for operational dispatch.
  • Coordinate and execute workflows related to booking, reminders, or general customer support.
  • Improve platform performance, security, and reliability.
  • Prevent service abuse, fraudulent actions, or standard Terms of Service violations.

4 SMS Communications

By contacting a business that uses RecoveryAI by phone or SMS, users may receive conversational SMS messages related to their inquiry, service request, booking request, or customer support interaction. RecoveryAI only sends conversational or operational messages related to customer interactions with businesses using the platform.

5 Data Sharing & Third-Party Restrictions

RecoveryAI does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Strict SMS Compliance Statement:
No mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in consent records, or consumer personal information will be shared, sold, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing, promotional, or advertising purposes under any circumstances.

We may share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • The Intended Business: The SMB service provider you initially called or messaged.
  • Essential Infrastructure Partners: Trusted technology providers required to operate the platform (such as core messaging systems, secure cloud hosting databases, and related cloud utilities).
  • Legal Requirements: Regulatory and legal authorities if we are strictly required to do so by applicable local, state, or federal laws.

6 Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards to help protect personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction. However, because no storage method or network transmission is completely impenetrable, we cannot guarantee 100% security of any transmitted data.

7 Data Retention

We retain user information only for as long as necessary to effectively deliver customer services, maintain accurate operational records, satisfy legal or audit requirements, resolve ongoing platform disputes, and safely improve the performance of our software product.

8 User Rights

Depending on your location and circumstances, you have the following basic user rights:

  • Request access to the personal data we store on you.
  • Request corrections to inaccurate personal records.
  • Request the secure deletion of your contact data.
  • Opt out of SMS communications completely by replying with the phrase STOP.

If you wish to submit an access, correction, or deletion request, please reach out directly via email to: getrecoveryai@gmail.com.
