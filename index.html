Overview

At RecoveryAI, we value your privacy and trust. RecoveryAI provides AI-powered conversational SMS communication, missed-call recovery, lead capture, customer follow-up, booking coordination, and customer support communication tools designed for SMB (Small and Medium-sized Businesses) partners.

Our platform helps businesses respond faster to customers, recover missed opportunities, improve communication workflows, and provide streamlined customer support through direct phone and SMS interactions.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

To provide our communication and workflow services effectively, we may collect the following information:

Contact Data

Name, phone number, and related customer contact details.

SMS Message Content

Full historical log of conversation content exchanged through the platform.

Call Metadata

Missed call timestamps, phone numbers, and related communication logs.

Service Request Details

Information related directly to booking requests, business inquiries, and support logs.

Preferences

Scheduling, booking, and communication preferences.

Business Configuration Data

Internal communication and workflow settings for businesses using RecoveryAI.

Website & Device Information

Browser type, device type, IP address, and anonymous website interaction analytics.

The information we collect is utilized strictly to operating, improve, and securing the RecoveryAI platform. Specifically, we process this detail to:

Respond to customer inquiries.
Send conversational follow-up messages.
Recover missed-call opportunities.
Coordinate appointment scheduling.
Deliver customer support communication.
Improve platform reliability & security.
Prevent fraud, abuse, or misuse of the platform, maintaining overall operational reporting analytics.
